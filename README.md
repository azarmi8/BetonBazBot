# BetonBazBot

> Concrete engineering AI tooling.

BetonBazBot is part of the **BetonBaz engineering software family**, focused on practical AI-assisted workflows for concrete and construction engineering.

## Position in the ecosystem

```text
Real engineering data
        ↓
   BetonBazBot
        ↓
AI-assisted workflow
        ↓
Human verification
        ↓
Engineering decision
```

## Principles

- Engineering facts must remain traceable.
- AI suggestions are not engineering authority.
- Ambiguous information must be reviewed rather than invented.
- Automation should reduce repetitive work without hiding the underlying evidence.
- No fake telemetry or unsupported claims.

## Related systems

- **Cretiq** — concrete engineering intelligence platform
- **Virtual Concrete Lab** — interactive concrete/materials testing simulation
- **CRETIQ 6044** — interactive Iranian ready-mixed concrete standard learning environment
- **Agent HQ** — cloud control plane for governed AI agents
